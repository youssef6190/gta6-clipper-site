GTA6 Clipper

GTA6 Clipper is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps publish short GTA 6 stream clips to the owner's own channels:

  • YouTube: GTA 6 Streamer Clips

Each clip credits the streamer it comes from and is made under that streamer's public clipping campaign. The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Google, YouTube, TikTok or Meta.

What the tool does when the owner connects an account:

  • uploads the owner's finished clips to the owner's own account;
  • reads view counts and the status of those uploaded clips, to see which clips performed well.
