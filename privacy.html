Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

GTA6 Clipper ("the app") is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to publish their own video clips to their own social media accounts. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

Only when the owner signs in through the official Google, TikTok or Meta sign-in screen and clicks Allow:

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly): uploads videos to the owner's channel; reads the owner's channel name and the view counts and status of the owner's own uploaded videos.
  • TikTok: sends the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts; reads basic profile info and the view counts of the owner's own videos.
  • Instagram: publishes the owner's videos to the owner's Instagram account and reads their view counts.

How data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sent to any server run by the app, because there is none.
  • Data is never sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app does not access data belonging to anyone other than the owner.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions (and the matching settings in TikTok and Instagram). Deleting the app's folder on the owner's computer deletes all stored data.
